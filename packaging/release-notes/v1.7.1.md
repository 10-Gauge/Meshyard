If you're on 1.7.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **Meshyard.exe** below.

## Paints

- **Liveries with their own decals now show them.** Some paints add artwork the stock paint doesn't have, like the Idris Sharktooth's shark mouth across the front hangar doors, the Invictus camo emblems, the Arrow's Headhunters markings and the Pyro graffiti. These parts were missing before; now they come with the paint. The first time you pick one of these paints it takes a few extra seconds (it needs Blender), and after that it's instant.
- **Sparkle and sheen.** Paints with a metallic fleck pattern (the Railen's pink, teal and grey sparkle) and an iridescent sheen now show them, so those liveries look different from each other.
- **No more glittery black hulls.** The Railen and other ships no longer show their internal damage structure through the hull as a mirror-like glitter.
