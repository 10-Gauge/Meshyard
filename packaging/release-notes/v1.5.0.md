If you're on 1.4.1, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## New

- **Paints.** Ships with liveries in the game get a **Paint** list. Pick one and the ship shows in it in the viewer, and prints and exports use it too.
- **Painted prints for Bambu Studio.** New **Paint colors** setting for 3MF prints: pick how many filaments you have (2 to 16) and the print comes out colored from the ship, hull, trim and markings, in that many colors. Bambu Studio opens it already painted with filaments 1 to N; the app shows which color goes in which slot.
- **See the peg before you print.** With a stand, the viewer shows where the peg will go as an orange socket, and moves it as you change **Peg forward** and **Peg right**.
- **Show on stand.** Turn it on to see the whole ship sitting on the stand your settings will make: the base, the post height and the display angle.
- **Flight mode** now also spreads folded wings on more ships.

## Better looking, faster viewer

- Models load with more detail and sharper textures. Very large ships keep a lighter version so they still open quickly; **Full detail** still loads everything.
- New studio lighting with reflections and soft shadows, so metal looks like metal.
- Big ships no longer lag: one large explorer went from about 7 to over 50 frames a second.
- Painted logos the game fills in at run time no longer show as white squares, and floating light flares are gone.

## Fixes across the whole fleet

Every ship and vehicle was checked in every pose, and a lot was fixed:

- **Parts showing the wrong shape.** On most ships some parts drew another part's shape: thruster covers stood up as giant slabs, fins and missile pods showed body pieces, dashboards showed chairs. Every part now has its own shape.
- **Extra copies and stray pieces.** Ships no longer load with copies of themselves inside them, and stray hull copies and pieces floating off the ship are gone.
- **Gun parts.** Gun barrels, housings and vents now sit the right way round.
- **Landing gear.** Gear up and gear down now work on ships that had it backwards or missing.
- **Weapons deployed** now also opens missile bays, nose guns, bomb bay doors and missile racks.
- **Doors and hatches** left open in the game files are closed, so hulls print without holes.
- Very long ships are no longer cut off in the viewer.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
