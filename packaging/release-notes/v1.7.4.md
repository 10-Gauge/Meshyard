If you're on 1.7.0 or newer, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **Meshyard.exe** below.

## Ship paints

- **Idris Sharktooth looks like the game.** The hull now shows the dark camo with grey tiger stripes in the right places, with crisp stripe edges and full detail instead of a blurry pattern.
- **Turrets, railgun and racks keep their own paint.** Mounted weapons and equipment now show their plain beige paint instead of picking up the hull pattern. The hangar doors still carry the shark mouth.
- **Decals sit cleanly on the hull.** Markings no longer flicker or disappear into the paint underneath.
