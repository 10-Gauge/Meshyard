If you're on 1.5.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## Stands

- **See the real stand before you export.** A few seconds after you change a stand setting, the viewer shows the stand the export will build: name plate, lettering, logo, posts and colors.
- **Base size and a second post.** New **Base width** and **Base depth** settings (or leave them at 0 to size from the model), a **Posts** option for two posts fore and aft on long ships, and a balance check that warns when the ship would tip over.
- **Stand colors for painted prints.** With Paint colors on, pick the base, logo, text and second line colors; they go into the 3MF as their own filaments.
- **New Tilt setting** banks the ship on its stand: right wing down toward the front of the stand, or left wing down away from it. **Display angle** is now called **Incline**.
- **Ships now sit nose-right on the stand**, seen from the front (the name plate side), so the plate reads under the ship's side.
- **Show on stand** now shows the stand from its front, with the front edge marked and FRONT on the floor, and the note under the settings spells out the nose direction, incline and tilt. The base no longer shows stood on end.
- The peg now stays on the ship's centerline where it can, in front of or behind a fin that hangs under the middle, and when it has to move sideways it moves toward the stand's front, so the post isn't hidden behind the fin.

## Paints

- **Liveries now show properly.** Before, a paint mostly changed the main hull color. Now each panel takes the color the livery gives it, so secondary and trim colors land where they belong, metallic paints like chrome, gold and red metal look metallic, and camo, stripe and pattern liveries show their pattern on the hull. The first time you pick a paint on a ship, the app takes a few seconds to read its liveries.
- **The stock paint is rebuilt too**, so ships show their real colors: bronze and silver metal trim no longer comes out as glittery black, and liveries that recolor hull panels now do. Bare metal looks like metal on every ship.
- **Painted prints** name the closest Bambu PLA Basic color for each filament slot, so you know which spool to load.
- A few ships' paints work differently in the game and aren't in the Paint list yet.

## Fixes

- **Updates no longer get stuck downloading.** If the connection goes quiet, the app now picks the download back up where it left off instead of sitting at 1%. If you're stuck on an older version, grab the exe below.
- **Landing gear up** now works on a group of older fighters and a few small ships. Before, their gear stayed down in the gear up view.
- **Landing gear up** now folds each leg away on ships with unusual gear, the way it does in the game.
- **Weapons deployed** now brings out only the cannons on ships with hidden guns: the top wing gun covers stay shut, the way they are in the game. Turning it off really stows the cannons again (Flight mode used to bring them out too).
- The peg preview no longer fails with "Access is denied" when the ship is still being posed for the viewer at the same time.
- The cockpit HUD no longer shows up as a blue pixel panel over the windshield, and cockpit screens show switched off instead of as a blue grid.
