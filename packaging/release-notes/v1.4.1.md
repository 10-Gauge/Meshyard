If you're on 1.4.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## Fixes

- **Gear down on first load.** Ships that land in a different shape, and ships whose game files have the gear up, now show the gear down the first time you open them, instead of only after raising the gear once. The first load of each ship takes a few seconds longer, once.
- **Landing gear struts.** With the gear down on ships that fold their wings into their legs, the struts now extend so the ship stands on its wheels instead of sitting on its belly.
- **Deployed mode.** Flight mode now brings out nose cannons on ships that deploy them, as the game does.
- **Stand peg.** The peg now stays clear of fins and thin parts under the ship, like a fin hanging under the hull. New **Peg forward** and **Peg right** settings in the print panel move it if you want it somewhere else.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
