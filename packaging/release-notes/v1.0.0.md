First numbered release. Download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below and double-click it. There's no installer.

## What it does

- **Browse** every ship, vehicle, weapon, prop and piece of armor in your own copy of the game, live or test builds, and look at it in 3D.
- **3D print** anything: one click gives you a watertight 3MF or STL at the size you pick, with thin parts thickened, an optional stand, and a round base or a name plate with raised lettering and the manufacturer's logo.
- **Export** models as GLB, glTF, OBJ, Collada, PLY, STL or 3MF, and through Blender as FBX, USDZ or `.blend`.
- **Landing gear up** and **weapons deployed** switches for ships, taken from each ship's own animations.
- **Characters**: build a figure from the armor and clothing in the game, pose it with the game's own stances, and hand it any gun in the game.
- **Blender sandbox**: spawn assets straight into a Blender scene, with working gear, doors and ramps.
- **Keeps up with patches**: a new game build is noticed and indexed on its own.

## Good to know

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with the game installed.
- The tools that read the game files (StarBreaker and cgf-converter) are inside the exe.
- Blender is a separate, free install. You only need it for the gear and weapon switches, FBX, USDZ and `.blend` export, and the sandbox. The app points you to the download if it can't find it.
- Windows SmartScreen will warn you the first time because the exe isn't code-signed. Click *More info*, then *Run anyway*.
- It only reads your game files. It never changes them, never touches the game client, and doesn't talk to any game servers.
- Everything you pull out of the game belongs to whoever made it. Keep it for your own use and don't share the files.
