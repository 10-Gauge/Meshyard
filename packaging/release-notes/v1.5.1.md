If you're on 1.5.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## Fixes

- **Updates no longer get stuck downloading.** If the connection goes quiet, the app now picks the download back up where it left off instead of sitting at 1%. If you're stuck on an older version, grab the exe below.
- **Landing gear up** now works on the original Hornets (F7A, F7C, F7C-M, F7C-R, F7C-S, Wildfire and Heartseeker), the Archimedes, the Skylark and the Intrepid. Before, their gear stayed down in the gear up view.
- **Landing gear up** now works on the Basher: each leg folds away the way it does in the game.
- **Weapons deployed** on the Stingray now brings out only its cannons: the top wing gun covers stay shut, the way they are in the game. Turning it off really stows the cannons again (Flight mode used to bring them out too).
- The peg preview no longer fails with "Access is denied" when the ship is still being posed for the viewer at the same time.
- The cockpit HUD no longer shows up as a blue pixel panel over the windshield (Alpha Wolf and others), and cockpit screens show switched off instead of as a blue grid.

## Painted prints

- For each filament slot, the app now names the closest Bambu PLA Basic color, so you know which spool to load.

## Paints

- **Liveries now show properly.** Before, a paint mostly changed the main hull color. Now each panel takes the color the livery gives it, so secondary and trim colors land where they belong, metallic paints like chrome, gold and red metal look metallic, and camo, stripe and pattern liveries show their pattern on the hull. The first time you pick a paint on a ship, the app takes a few seconds to read its liveries.
- **The stock paint is rebuilt too**, so ships like the Railen show their real colors: its bronze and silver metal trim no longer comes out as glittery black, and liveries that recolor its hull panels (White Grey Gold, Gold Grey Grey) now do. Bare metal looks like metal on every ship.
- The 300 series, Apollo and Hermes paints work differently in the game and aren't in the Paint list yet.

## Stands

- **New Tilt setting** banks the ship on its stand: right wing down toward the front of the stand, or left wing down away from it. **Display angle** is now called **Incline**.
- **Ships now sit nose-right on the stand**, seen from the front (the name plate side), so the plate reads under the ship's side.
- The peg now stays on the ship's centerline where it can, in front of or behind a fin that hangs under the middle, and when it has to move sideways it moves toward the stand's front, so the post isn't hidden behind the fin.
- **Show on stand** now shows the stand from its front, with the front edge marked and FRONT on the floor, and the note under the settings spells out the nose direction, incline and tilt. The base no longer shows stood on end.
