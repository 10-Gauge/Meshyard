If you're on 1.1.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## What's new

- **Scale on the name plate.** Turn on *Scale on the plate* and the print scale (like 1:85) goes on the plate's second line.
- **Print log.** Every print export is saved to `print-log.csv` in your exports folder. Pick something you've printed before and the print panel tells you when and at what scale. *Print history* lists everything.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
