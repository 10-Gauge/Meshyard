If you're on 1.7.0 or newer, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **Meshyard.exe** below.

## Printing

- **Refresh stand.** A new button under **Show on stand** rebuilds the stand in the viewer with your latest name plate text, logo, colors and base size, so you can check it before exporting.
- **Show on stand loads a fresh stand.** Turning it on now builds the stand again with the current settings, so you never see an old one.
- **Clearer stand messages.** If the stand preview can't be built, the note under the switch now says so instead of staying blank.
