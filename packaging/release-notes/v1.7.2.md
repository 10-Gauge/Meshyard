If you're on 1.7.0 or 1.7.1, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **Meshyard.exe** below.

The first time you open each ship after updating, it loads about as slowly as the very first time. Its model is rebuilt once with the fixes below.

## Paints

- **Liveries look more like the game.** Hull panels now take their own shade of the paint, so a livery shows its light, mid and dark panels instead of one flat color.
- **Camo paints show their pattern.** Camo and stripe liveries now paint their pattern in the livery's own colors, sharper than before. The Idris Sharktooth now has its dark hull with beige tiger stripes, and the Avenger's grey, black and red camo shows its three greys.
- **Tinted windows look like glass.** Liveries with tinted glass no longer turn the windows into flat colored panels.

## Printing

- **Painted Bambu prints keep their colors.** Bambu Studio now opens a painted 3MF in the ship's real colors and offers to match them to your filaments. Before, it filled the paint with whatever filaments your printer had loaded.
- **No more blotchy paint.** Colors you couldn't tell apart now share one filament, so wings and hulls no longer come out speckled in two.
- **The stand preview shows your plate text.** The stand in the viewer updates when you type the name plate text, change the logo or turn the scale on or off.
- **The up and down buttons on number fields close again.** Clicking anywhere else in the side panel closes them.

## Ships

- **Idris turrets and point defense guns are whole again.** The remote turrets have their bodies back, and the point defense turrets have their own short barrels instead of a large laser cannon.
- **Turret gun mounts use their own parts.** The bigger gun mounts no longer borrow pieces from smaller ones.
