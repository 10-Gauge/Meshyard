If you're on 1.2.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## What's new

- **Ships change shape on the ground.** With the landing gear down, ships that land in a different shape now show it, folding their wings, standing on them, or moving engines with the gear. The shape is worked out the first time you raise the gear on a ship.
- **VTOL mode.** A new switch swings the thrusters down for vertical flight, using each ship's own animation.
- **Flight mode.** A new switch shows ships that fly in a different shape, with flaps opened or wings stood upright or spread out.

## Fixes

- **Wrong engines.** Some large cargo ships had big engines from another ship sticking out under the hull and into the cargo bay where their own small thrusters belong. They now show the right thrusters, and the same fix puts the right parts back on a few more ships.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
