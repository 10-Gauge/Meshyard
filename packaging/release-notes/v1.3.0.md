If you're on 1.2.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## What's new

- **Ships change shape on the ground.** With the landing gear down, ships that land in a different shape now show it: the Eclipse, Stingray, Talon, Vanguard and Zeus fold their wings, the Clipper stands on its wings, and the Mole, Moth, Golem, Pitbull, Apollo, Hermes and others move their engines or wings with the gear. The shape is worked out the first time you raise the gear on a ship.
- **VTOL mode.** A new switch swings the thrusters down for vertical flight, using each ship's own animation: the Starlifter's wing nacelles, the Paladin, Hull B, Golem, RAFT, Starlancer, Prowler Utility and more.
- **Flight mode.** A new switch shows ships that fly in a different shape: the Stingray opens its flaps, the Reliant stands its wings upright and the Corsair spreads them.

## Fixes

- **Starlifter engines.** The A2, C2 and M2 Hercules had large engines from another ship sticking out under the hull and into the cargo bay where their own small thrusters belong. They now show the right thrusters. The same fix puts the right parts back on the Carrack, Hull A and MPUV-1T.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
