If you're on 1.3.0, the app offers this update when you open it. Otherwise download **SC-Asset-Browser.exe** below.

## What's new

- **More ship shapes.** Ships whose outer wings fold into their landing legs now show that with the gear down, as they do in the game, and flight mode draws the wings in, raises the wingtip flaps and lowers the fin under the ship.
- **Lowered rifle stance.** The low ready pose now holds a rifle across the body, stock at the chest and muzzle pointed down to the side, the way characters stand in the game.
- **Weapons on the back.** Rifles hang straight down the spine with the stock up. Large weapons like railguns hang on the right with the muzzle up.

## Fixes

- **Front hand.** When aiming, the support hand now wraps around the front of the gun instead of hovering with the thumb up.
- **Turret guns in prints.** Guns that sit with a small gap on their turret are joined to the model, so they no longer come out as loose parts.
- **Game poses on a fresh install.** The character builder now uses the game's own stances the first time, instead of falling back to the built-in ones.
- **Ship poses refresh.** Landing gear and wing poses worked out by an older version are redone automatically.

The [README](https://github.com/10-Gauge/SC-Asset-Browser#readme) has the full walkthrough.
